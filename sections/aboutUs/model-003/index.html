We build for the people who will inherit the building.

BC is a practice of eleven architects working from one long room in Porto. Since 1998 we have finished nineteen buildings, most of them in cast‑in‑place concrete, and every one of them is still in use.

We place daylight first and the programme second. The programme will change several times over a building’s life; the path of the sun across the site will not.

How we work, written as a specification.

Office standard, revision 7. Issued March 2026. Binding on every project we sign.

  1. 1.1

    Daylight is placed before rooms.

    We model a year of sun across the site before a single wall is drawn. Rooms are then fitted to the light, not the other way round.

  2. 1.2

    Structure is poured once and left exposed.

    No cladding over concrete that can carry its own finish. The board marks from the formwork are the finish, and the budget saved goes into the windows.

  3. 1.3

    Every building ships with a fifty-year manual.

    Handed over with the keys and written for the caretaker, not the client: what to clean, what to seal, and what never to paint.

  4. 1.4

    We return in the fifth winter.

    Each finished building is visited when it has been cold and wet for a while. What we got wrong, we repair at our own cost.

  5. 1.5

    No more than four projects on the boards.

    A practice of eleven can know four buildings well. When a fifth arrives, it waits, or we recommend someone we trust.

A building is a promise made to strangers. Most of them have not been born yet, so we try to keep it simply.
